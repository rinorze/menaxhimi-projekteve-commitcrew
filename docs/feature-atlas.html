Every feature of the app, from the first screen to the last setting, each with a screen mockup. It is a travel and discovery app that feels like social media without posts, likes or comments: you go to real places, unlock them with your camera, and explore all 38 municipalities with your friends.

Who builds what

Beginners get Easy screens (forms, lists, cards) built from the shared components. Rinor and Elios take the Hard parts: maps, camera, real-time, AI and the backend. The lists show each person's MVP features.

Mockups are drawn in code from the design kit; photos are illustrations, not real images. Features and roles follow docs/PLAN.md. Research and screen drafts were written with Grok 4.7 and Cursor Composer, then reviewed.
