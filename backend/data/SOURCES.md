# Data sources

- `places.json` (318 places), `municipalities.json` (38), `regions.json` (7): OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), Wikidata (CC0) and Wikipedia summaries (CC BY-SA). Each place keeps its `osm_url`, `wikidata` and `summary_url`.
- `events.json`: official event websites; `tentative: true` means the dates are not confirmed yet.
- `quests.json`: written by the team.
